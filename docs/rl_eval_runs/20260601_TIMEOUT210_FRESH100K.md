## Decision

210s timeout bottleneck was not confirmed by this fresh run.

- The 100K fresh training run never reached timeout and never killed the boss: `143/143 player_dead`.
- Eval210 ONNX also ended `5/5 player_dead`, with no timeout and no boss kill.
- Eval boss damage is far below the `40` threshold from the requested decision tree: average `7`, max `9`.
- Out-of-range/danger attacks did not recur: `attack_out_of_range=0`, attack on warning/damage/recent danger all `0`.
- The model attacks accurately when it attacks (`hit_rate=100%`), but opportunity use is low (`23.973%` in Eval210) and survival collapses early.
- Compared with the earlier 90s 100K training log (`avg boss_damage 22.92`, `max 36`) and the earlier ONNX sweep mismatch, this fresh 210s training did not improve the training policy and did not solve ONNX inference weakness.

Recommended next step:

1. Do not promote or extend `BossPPO_Timeout210_Fresh100k_v1` as-is.
2. Isolate policy export/runtime mismatch before more reward tuning:
   - Build an Eval210 scene with `BehaviorType=Default` and no embedded ONNX.
   - Run `mlagents-learn --inference --resume` from the checkpoint for both `BossPPO_SafeActionMask_100k_v1` and `BossPPO_Timeout210_Fresh100k_v1`.
   - If Python checkpoint inference matches training logs while ONNX remains weak, focus on ONNX/export/InferenceOnly action selection.
3. Separately verify `PlayerDeathPenalty` plumbing before reward tuning:
   - `BossRLReward.cs` still declares and applies `PlayerDeathPenalty = -8.0f`.
   - However current and previous player_dead episode summaries show `death=0.000`.
   - This may mean death terminal reward is not being observed on the final decision step. Fixing it would be a gameplay/reward-affecting change, so it was not changed in this run.
